A2R Toolbox — Help
What is Stacking?

Troop stacking is a technique in Total Battle where you send multiple tiers of troops together to maximize damage while minimizing losses. By using lower-tier troops to fill your capacity, retraining becomes significantly cheaper.

The goal is to outnumber the layers of the Epic monster to secure more guaranteed hits. Use stacking for repeated Epic attacks to ensure consistent results and stable rewards.

If you go "all-in" with only your strongest tier, you might occasionally see higher rewards, but it isn't guaranteed because of how the combat system works. You will also lose more units, and retraining best-tier units requires more silver. Stacking is a sustainable approach that saves resources and ensures you get consistent rewards.

How the calculator works
  • Leadership: March capacity for Guardsmen, Specialists, and Catapults. This sets the "damage goal" those units balance toward.
  • Authority: How many Mercenaries you can bring. With Leadership set, mercenaries scale to match your stack's damage (like monsters). With no Leadership but Authority filled in, capacity is split across selected mercenaries only.
  • Selection: Pick the units to include. The calculator balances the stack so every selected tier deals the same effective damage per soldier.
  • Monsters & Dominance:
    • Mirror mode: With Leadership entered, monsters scale to match your guards' damage.
    • Pure monster mode: With no Leadership but Dominance entered, capacity is split across selected monsters only.
    The icon appears when you enter Dominance or Authority and the balanced stack needs more capacity than you have—it shows the maximum that still keeps the mix in balance.
  • Damage formula: For Guardsmen, Specialists, Monsters, and Catapults, Army Bonuses and unit-card features are included: base × (1 + Strength bonuses + feature bonus). Strength percents add (they are not multiplied). The feature line uses the highest Melee / Ranged / Mounted / Flying % on the unit card that applies—when an Epic target is set, only types present on that epic count. Fortification, beast, siege, and similar lines are ignored. Mercenaries use base damage only for now—we do not have enough in-game data to apply mercenary bonuses reliably (mercenary bonus inputs are planned for a future update).
  • Epic target (optional): Pick Standard (preset encounters) or Custom (event epics: one layer per combat type). This steers feature % and unlocks epic kill estimates. Expand Epic squad details for enemy strength, HP, and features (reference only). Standard and Custom epic targets are saved with presets and share codes (v3+).
  • Army Bonuses (optional): On each unit tab, expand Army Bonuses at the top. Enter Strength against Epic % once—it syncs across all tabs. Then enter that tab’s Strength % grid (Guardsmen, Specialists, Monsters) or Catapult Strength %. Those numbers already include hero talents, research, equipment, etc.; do not enter them twice. Accordions close when you switch views or load a preset. Mercenary bonus rows are planned once their in-game rules are clear.
Citadel siege

Use this mode to plan an opening catapult volley against citadel walls—not Epic marches.

  • Toolbox mode: Choose Citadel siege at the top. Leadership, Authority, Dominance, Epic target, and non-catapult unit tabs are hidden.
  • Walls: Enter how many walls you need to break. Goal and combined volley show in the citadel panel and in Siege summary (FAB, bottom-right).
  • Catapults: On the Catapults tab, your highest catapult tier (recommended) gives the exact count to break all walls. Multiple tiers use the same damage-per-tier rule as epic march stacking (shared volley goal), which may recommend more catapults than a single tier. Per-tier volley is on each card; use the FAB for Siege summary.
  • Damage: Per-catapult strike uses ×20 vs fortification, the Fortification % on the unit card, and Catapult Strength % from the Catapults tab Army Bonuses accordion.
  • Presets: Saved presets keep selected catapults, stats, bonuses, and epic target, but not citadel mode or wall count—you still switch mode and re-enter walls after loading.
Detail & Summary views

Use the floating button (bottom-right) to switch views. Calculations update whenever stats, units, bonuses, or epic target change.

  • Detail: Select units by category and tier; see troop counts and total stack damage on each card.
  • Summary: All selected units grouped by tier (highest tier first). Within each tier, rows are sorted by stack damage (highest first).
  • Dice icon: Marks stacks tied for the highest total damage in your army (~50% chance that stack is eliminated first in battle). This is a rule-of-thumb, not a combat simulation.
  • Epic selected: Summary shows estimated kills per stack (best layer by stack damage). warns if a stack may not reach one enemy HP on any layer. Check your battle journal if numbers look off.
Presets

Presets store stat inputs, selected units, Army Bonuses, and the active epic target in this browser only (local storage)—nothing is uploaded.

  • Load: Pick a name in the dropdown to apply it and open Summary view.
  • Reset: Clears stats and unit selections, returns the dropdown to -- Load Preset --, and opens Detail view. Saved presets are unchanged.
  • Save: Enabled once at least one of Leadership, Authority, or Dominance has a value. Use Update to overwrite the selected preset or save under a new name.
  • Delete: Removes the selected preset after confirmation.
  • Export / Import: Export needs a preset selected; copies an a2r-preset: share code (v3 includes bonuses and epic target). Import adds Imported: … and loads it. Older v1/v2 codes import without bonuses and/or epic.
Pro-Tips
  • Below G3? Just send your best troops and mercenaries; don't worry about stacking yet.
  • Optimal Stack: Select your 2–3 best Guardsman tiers. Adding monsters from the same tiers is a great way to increase damage without increasing Leadership costs.
  • Note on Damage: Adding more types of troops lowers the total damage of each individual slot. The goal is for every type of unit to secure kills. Check your in-game journal; if a tier shows 0 kills, try selecting fewer types or tiers.
  • Epic kill warning: When an epic target is set and a unit shows this warning, that stack may not deal enough damage to kill at least one enemy on any layer. Try removing your lowest-damage units (or tiers) until every remaining stack can secure kills—or swap in stronger units, or use a captain with higher Leadership so your main tiers can carry more troops.
  • Mercenaries: You can add more than the recommended amount. If you're lucky, they can secure extra kills, but it is not guaranteed.
