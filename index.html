Guide: Troop Stacking
What is Stacking?

Troop stacking is a technique in Total Battle where you send multiple tiers of troops together to maximize damage while minimizing losses. By using lower-tier troops to fill your capacity, retraining becomes significantly cheaper.

The goal is to outnumber the layers of the Epic monster to secure more guaranteed hits. Use stacking for repeated Epic attacks to ensure consistent results and stable rewards.

If you go "all-in" with only your strongest tier, you might occasionally see higher rewards, but it isn't guaranteed because of how the combat system works. You will also lose more units, and retraining best-tier units requires more silver. Stacking is a sustainable approach that saves resources and ensures you get consistent rewards.

How the calculator works
  • Leadership: Enter your total troop capacity for the march to set the "Damage Goal" for your army.
  • Selection: Pick the units to include. The calculator automatically balances the stack so every tier deals equal damage.
  • Monsters & Dominance:
    • Mirror Mode: If Leadership is entered, monsters automatically scale to match your Guardsmen's damage.
    • Pure Monster Mode: If no Leadership is provided but Dominance is entered, the app distributes that capacity equally across all selected monsters.
    Note: The ⚠️ warning icon only appears if you provide your Dominance. It alerts you if the "optimal" damage match exceeds what your current dominance can actually hold.
  • Damage & stacking logic: Each tier is balanced so every selected stack deals the same effective damage. With no Army Bonuses entered, that uses each unit's base damage from the database. When you fill in bonuses, counts use base × (1 + Strength bonuses + highest Melee/Ranged/Mounted/Flying feature on the card) — Strength percents add (they are not multiplied). Fortification, beast, siege, and similar lines on the card are ignored for now.
  • Army Bonuses (optional): Expand the accordion under the stat inputs. Enter the Strength % the game shows (e.g. 52 for 52%) — Strength against Epic, then your Guardsman / Specialist / Monster grid (Melee, Ranged, Mounted, Flying). Those values already include hero talents, research, equipment, and other bonuses baked into your in-game Strength; you do not enter them separately. The accordion closes when you switch views or load a preset. Catapult and Mercenary bonus inputs are planned for a future update. Health % fields are shown but not used in counts yet.
Presets

Presets remember your stat inputs, selected units, and Army Bonuses values. They are stored only in this browser (local storage on your device)—nothing is uploaded to a server.

  • Load: Choose a name in the dropdown to apply that preset and open Summary view.
  • Reset: Clears all stat fields and unit selections, returns the dropdown to -- Load Preset -- (no preset selected), and switches to Detail view. Saved presets in storage are unchanged.
  • Save: Available once at least one stat field has a value. Use Update when a preset is selected to overwrite it or save under a new name.
  • Export / Import: Export (with a preset selected) copies a share code to your clipboard. Import pastes a code and adds it as a new entry named Imported: … so you can back up stacks or share them with others.
Pro-Tips
  • Below G3? Just send your best troops and mercenaries; don't worry about stacking yet.
  • Optimal Stack: Select your 2–3 best Guardsman tiers. Adding monsters from the same tiers is a great way to increase damage without increasing Leadership costs.
  • Note on Damage: Adding more types of troops lowers the total damage of each individual slot. The goal is for every type of unit to secure kills. Check your in-game journal; if a tier shows 0 kills, try selecting fewer types or tiers.
  • Mercenaries: You can add more than the recommended amount. If you're lucky, they can secure extra kills, but it is not guaranteed.
